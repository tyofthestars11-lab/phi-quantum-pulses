  Y_6^0(theta_t) = sqrt(13/(4*pi)) * P_6(-1/3)
                   = 1.0171072362820548 * 0.1934156378600823
                   = 0.1967244448775991

================================================================================
SECTION 7: SPIRAL-BASED COORDINATE TRANSFORMATIONS
          FOR SCROLL FIBER MAPPING
================================================================================

SCROLL GEOMETRY: The papyrus scroll is modeled as a golden spiral
in polar coordinates, unwinding from center outward.

TRANSFORMATION 1: Polar Golden Spiral to Cartesian
  r(theta) = a * phi^(theta/pi)
  x(theta) = r(theta) * cos(theta) = a * phi^(theta/pi) * cos(theta)
  y(theta) = r(theta) * sin(theta) = a * phi^(theta/pi) * sin(theta)

TRANSFORMATION 2: Arc length along golden spiral
  ds = sqrt(r^2 + (dr/dtheta)^2) * d_theta

  dr/dtheta = a * phi^(theta/pi) * ln(phi) / pi
  ln(phi) = 0.4812118250596035
  ln(phi)/pi = 0.1531744812650166

  Let k = ln(phi)/pi = 0.1531744812650166
  Then r(theta) = a * e^(k*theta)
  dr/dtheta = a * k * e^(k*theta) = k * r(theta)

  ds/dtheta = r * sqrt(1 + k^2)
            = r * sqrt(1 + 0.0234624217108069)
            = r * 1.0116631957874156
